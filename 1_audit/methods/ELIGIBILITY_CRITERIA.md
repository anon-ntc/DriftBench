# Paper eligibility criteria

A paper was included when at least one evaluation central to its principal contribution met every requirement below.

- The classifier-facing input consisted of passively observable network packets, flows, sessions, traffic traces, or features computed from them.
- The prediction method was learned from data.
- The evaluation used a fixed label scheme with at least two classes.
- Each evaluated sample received one label from the relevant scheme.
- The classification evaluation was central to the paper rather than an ancillary component.
- The publication was a full archival research paper within the venue and year frame.

Encrypted and unencrypted traffic were both eligible. Eligible task families included application, service, website, device, user, activity, protocol, intrusion, malware, botnet, attack, and other semantic traffic classifications when the requirements above were satisfied.

The review excluded non-full publications, studies without qualifying network-traffic input, methods without a learned classifier, clustering-only or one-class analyses, rule or signature systems, methods dependent on active probing, defense-only work without a separable qualifying classification evaluation, adversarial or generative support tasks, purely multilabel tasks, operational transport or routing labels, surveys, systematizations, replications, and benchmark or framework papers without a central qualifying classifier.

Mixed papers were included if at least one central evaluation qualified. Only qualifying evaluations contributed to the analytical coding tables.

The unit at this stage is a publication record. Dataset-specific class schemes and materially distinct evaluation designs are defined separately in the coding documentation.
